# Welcome to The Ministry of Faith / House of Hope

Led by **Pastor Don Jones** and **Minister Barbara Jones**, The Ministry of Faith — also known as the House of Hope — is a spirit-filled church family rooted in the love of Jesus Christ, right here in Houston, Texas.

We believe that every person carries God-given purpose. Our doors are open to all who are seeking healing, community, and a deeper walk with the Lord.

*"For I know the plans I have for you," declares the Lord, "plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future."*
-- Jeremiah 29:11

---

## What We Believe

We are a family of believers committed to faith, community, service, and hope. We trust God in every season of life, walk together and lift one another up, reach the lost and hurting, and share the Good News that transforms lives.

---

## Our Ministries

**M.E.N.S. — Meeting Every Need Spiritually**
Our men's ministry building strong men of faith in Houston.

**P.M.O.T. — Prison Ministry Outreach Team**
Carrying the Gospel into correctional facilities and restoring hope to the incarcerated.
