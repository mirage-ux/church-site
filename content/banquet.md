## Annual Banquet

Join us for an unforgettable evening of worship, fellowship, and celebration as The Ministry of Faith / House of Hope gathers together for our Annual Banquet. This is a special night set apart to give God glory for all He has done — and all He has yet to do — in the lives of our church family and the Houston community.

---

## A Night to Remember

The Annual Banquet is one of the most cherished events in our church calendar. From the moment you walk through the doors, you will experience the warmth and love that defines the House of Hope family — great food, heartfelt worship, powerful testimonies, and the joy of being together in the presence of the Lord.

Come ready to be encouraged, uplifted, and inspired.
