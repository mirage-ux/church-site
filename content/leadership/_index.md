## Our Ministries

At The Ministry of Faith / House of Hope, we believe the church is called to go beyond its four walls and serve people where they are. Under the leadership of Pastor Don Jones and Minister Barbara Jones, our ministries reach into the hearts of men, families, and those society has often forgotten.

---

## M.E.N.S. — Meeting Every Need Spiritually

**Men's Ministry**

M.E.N.S. is our dedicated men's ministry, built on the conviction that when men are grounded in God, families are strengthened and communities are transformed.

The M.E.N.S. ministry provides a space where men can grow in faith through Bible study, prayer, and accountability — developing godly character as husbands, fathers, and leaders. Men support one another through life's challenges with honesty and brotherhood, and serve the community as men who reflect the love and strength of Christ.

M.E.N.S. meets regularly and welcomes all men — whether you are a longtime believer or are just beginning your walk with God. There is a place for you here.

---

## P.M.O.T. — Prison Ministry Outreach Team

**Reaching the Incarcerated with the Gospel**

The Prison Ministry Outreach Team reflects one of the most foundational calls of the Gospel — to visit the imprisoned, bring hope to the hopeless, and remind every man and woman behind bars that God has not forgotten them.

Pastor Don Jones and the P.M.O.T. team carry the message of salvation, redemption, and new life into correctional facilities throughout the region. We believe that no wall can contain the love of God, and that every person — regardless of what they have done — is worthy of hearing the Good News of Jesus Christ.

Our prison ministry includes worship services inside correctional facilities, Bible study and discipleship programs for inmates, reentry support to help those released rebuild their lives in Christ, and personal prayer and counseling for those in need.

If you feel called to this ministry or know someone who needs to hear the Gospel behind bars, please reach out to us.
