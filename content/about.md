## About The Ministry of Faith / House of Hope

The Ministry of Faith — the House of Hope — is a Christ-centered church family in Houston, Texas. Founded on the belief that God's Word has the power to transform lives, we are committed to preaching the Gospel, making disciples, and serving our community with love and compassion.

We are a church for everyone. No matter where you come from, what you have been through, or where you are in your spiritual journey — you are welcome here.

---

## Our Pastors

### Pastor Don Jones

Pastor Don Jones is a devoted servant of God, a shepherd of souls, and a pillar of the Houston community. Called to ministry with a heart for the broken and the lost, Pastor Don has dedicated his life to proclaiming the saving grace of Jesus Christ and building a church where every person is known, loved, and valued.

His passion is people — meeting them where they are and helping them discover the hope and purpose that only God can give. Through preaching, discipleship, and hands-on ministry, Pastor Don has touched countless lives throughout Houston and beyond.

### Minister Barbara Jones

Minister Barbara Jones stands faithfully alongside Pastor Don as a gifted leader and minister in her own right. Her warmth, wisdom, and dedication to the Word of God make her a beloved figure in the congregation and the broader community.

Minister Barbara pours herself into the women and families of the church, offering guidance, encouragement, and a steady presence that reflects the heart of Christ.

Together, Pastor Don and Minister Barbara lead with unity, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to God's calling on their lives.

---

## Our Mission

*"To love God, love people, and serve the world — one life at a time."*

We exist to preach the Word of God without compromise, welcome all people into a loving church family, disciple believers to grow in their faith, reach into our community with the hope of the Gospel, and care for the hurting, the incarcerated, and the underserved.
